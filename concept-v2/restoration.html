Know your rights

Your insurance company doesn't choose who rebuilds your home. You do.

Your insurer can't tell you which contractor you have to use. The decision is yours, and you can hire any licensed contractor you trust to repair your home.

That choice matters. Insurance estimates are often written with pricing that doesn't match current local costs, and in Interior and rural Alaska, materials, freight and labor often cost more than in the Lower 48. When an estimate comes in short, or damage gets missed, the homeowner can end up covering the gap, sometimes to the tune of thousands of dollars.

What sets Edstrom apart

We work for you, the insured, not the insurance company. We stand in your corner to make sure your claim reflects the full cost of restoring your home the right way, at today's Alaska prices.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to use my insurance company's contractor?

No. You choose who repairs your home. You can hire any licensed contractor you trust, including Edstrom.

What if the insurance estimate seems too low?

Send it to us. We compare it against what we document on site and our line-item estimate at current Alaska pricing. If items were missed or underpriced, we submit a supplement request to your adjuster with photos and documentation. Your insurance company makes the final coverage decision.

What will I have to pay?

You're responsible for your deductible, which is set by your policy. For covered repairs, our goal is that your deductible is your only out-of-pocket cost. If something isn't covered, or you choose an upgrade, we'll price it in writing before any work is done.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the damage, insurance approvals, material and freight lead times, and the season. We start drying as soon as we're on site. Once the scope is approved, we give you a rebuild schedule and keep you updated through the end of the job.
