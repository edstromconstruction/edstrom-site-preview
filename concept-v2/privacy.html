Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how Edstrom Construction Company, LLC handles information collected through this website. We keep it short and in plain English.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Edstrom Construction Company, LLC ("Edstrom," "we," "us"), a general contractor based in North Pole, Alaska. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Quote and estimate requests. When you use the quote form, we collect what you enter: your name, phone number, email address, project type, project location and project details. Form submissions are delivered to our office email through a third-party form-processing service (currently FormSubmit).
  • Calls and email. If you call us or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and anything you choose to tell us, such as your address, photos, plans or insurance claim details.
  • Website chat assistant. We plan to add a chat assistant to this website. It is an automated assistant powered by artificial intelligence, not a person. If you use it, it may ask for your name, phone number and project details so our office can follow up, and the conversation may be saved as a transcript. The chat assistant cannot give legal, insurance or claim advice, and its answers are not a quote or a contract.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit and the date and time of your visit.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies. We may use Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Customer portal

The Customer Login link takes you to a separate customer portal service. Information you enter there is handled under that service's own terms and privacy practices, not this policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To prepare estimates and proposals.
  • To provide our construction and restoration services and manage your project.
  • To operate, maintain and improve this website.
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run our business and this website, such as our email, website hosting, form-processing and chat tools. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Your project. If you ask us to, we may share information needed for your project, for example with your insurance adjuster.
  • Legal requirements. When required by law, court order or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Edstrom, our customers or others.

5. Text messages

We do not send marketing text messages unless you have separately given us your express consent to receive them. Giving us your phone number in the quote form does not sign you up for marketing texts. We may call or text you about a request you made or a project you hired us for.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, including to follow up on your request, perform and support your project, keep business and tax records, and meet legal requirements. After that, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of sending or storing information over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please don't send sensitive information such as bank account or Social Security numbers through the website form or chat.

8. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we have about you by emailing office@edstromak.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we may keep some information when the law requires it or when we need it for an active project or our business records.

10. Other websites

This website may link to other websites, such as Google Maps or our customer portal. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
