Local know-how

Remodeling in a cold-climate house

Opening walls in an Interior Alaska home means dealing with vapor retarders, insulation and heating lines. We put the vapor barrier back right, insulate what we open and coordinate licensed electrical and plumbing work where the remodel needs it, so the finished room is as warm as it looks.

Questions

Straight answers.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

It depends on scope and materials. Cabinet and counter lead times often set the schedule. We give you a timeline with the estimate.

Can you remodel in winter?

Yes. Interior remodels work year-round. We protect the rest of the house from dust and keep heat on in the work area.

Do you pull permits?

Where a permit is required for your location and scope, we handle it as the contractor. Requirements differ inside and outside city limits.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical?

We coordinate licensed electrical and plumbing contractors for those parts of a remodel and manage the schedule.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster, and an estimate is not a guarantee of final cost.
