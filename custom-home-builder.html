Since 2010

Built for Interior Alaska

Edstrom Construction has built in Interior Alaska since 2010, with more than $40 million in work performed. We build custom homes and multifamily buildings in North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright: site prep, foundation and in-floor heat, framing, insulation, roofing, tile, millwork and finish carpentry, with one project manager from the first meeting to the keys.

Short building seasons, deep cold and freight lead times shape every schedule. We plan foundations around your site's soils, including permafrost where it's present, and we plan material orders and enclosure so interior work can continue into winter.

Questions

New home questions

How much does it cost to build a house in Fairbanks or North Pole?

It depends on size, site work, foundation, heat system and finishes. Freight and the building season affect price too. We give a detailed estimate and schedule after reviewing your plans and lot. An estimate is not a guarantee of final cost.

Do you build from my plans?

Yes. Edstrom offers design-build. Send your plans or sketches to office@edstromak.com and a project manager will review them, or tell us what you want and we'll work through the design with you.

Where do you build?

North Pole, Fairbanks, Fort Wainwright and other Interior communities. Ask about remote sites.
