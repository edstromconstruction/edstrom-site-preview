What we do

Remediation that deals with the moisture.

Removing visible mold and leaving the leak, or closing a wet wall, does not finish the job. We correct the moisture and rebuild what we open. For the leak itself, see water damage restoration or frozen and burst pipe repair.

Find the moistureWe look for the leak, backup, burst pipe or cold-wall condensation that is feeding the mold, including behind finishes.
Remove damaged materialMold-damaged drywall, insulation and carpet that cannot be cleaned come out. Surfaces that can be cleaned are cleaned.
Dry before you closeWe dry the framing and cavities and check moisture before new drywall or flooring goes on.
Repair the openingWe rebuild the areas we opened. If you want a separate environmental test, an independent consultant can do that. We do the remediation and the repairs.

Insurance claims

Some mold is a claim. Some is not.

Mold that follows a covered water loss may be part of that claim. Mold from long-term humidity or maintenance often is not. Your insurer decides. When the work is a claim, Edstrom documents the moisture and the affected material and bills your insurance company directly.

When it is not a claim, we still give you a written price before we start. We do not tell you a policy covers mold, and we are not a public adjuster. You choose the contractor either way.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. A direct bill is not a promise that a claim will be paid, and an estimate is not a coverage decision. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster. If something is not covered, or you want an upgrade, we price that work in writing before we do it.

We bill insurance directly

We bill your insurance company directly when mold remediation is part of a claim. We do not guarantee that the policy covers mold.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you provide mold remediation in Fairbanks and North Pole?

Yes. Edstrom Construction remediates mold tied to moisture in Fairbanks, North Pole and Interior Alaska, and we repair the areas we open.

Are you IICRC certified?

Yes. Edstrom Construction is IICRC certified. Our Alaska contractor license is CONG35843.

Does insurance pay for mold remediation?

Sometimes. Mold that follows a covered water loss may be part of the claim. Mold from ongoing humidity or maintenance often is not. Your insurer decides. We bill covered work directly. If it is not a claim, we give you a written price before we start.

Will you fix the leak that caused the mold?

Yes. Mold remediation includes the moisture source, whether that is a leak, a backup, or a frozen or burst pipe. Drying is part of the job.
