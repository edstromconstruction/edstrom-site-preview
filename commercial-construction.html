Scope

What we build

Edstrom Construction is a North Pole general contractor with more than $40 million in work performed in Interior Alaska and beyond. We build new commercial buildings, tenant improvements and renovations, multifamily and public projects, and we plan freight, barge windows and crew travel for remote sites before we mobilize.

  • New commercial buildings
  • Tenant improvements and renovations
  • Multifamily
  • Government and public facilities
  • Shops, pole barns and storage buildings
  • Restoration of commercial buildings after water or fire damage
