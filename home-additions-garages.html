Edstrom Construction builds room additions, attached and detached garages and shops for homeowners in North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright. We pour the foundation, frame, insulate, roof, side and finish, and one project manager runs it from the first site visit to the walkthrough.

Living space

Room additions

Bedrooms, family rooms, mudrooms and second stories. We tie the addition into the existing roof, walls and heat so it looks and works like it was always there.

Garages

Attached and detached garages

Single, double and oversized garages with the slab, insulation and door you need for an Interior winter. In-floor heat is an option. We pour it on our own homes and fourplexes.

Shops

Shops and pole barns

Questions

Addition and garage questions

How much does a home addition or garage cost in Fairbanks?

It depends on size, foundation, heat and finish level. After a site visit we give you a written, line-item estimate. Estimates are not a guarantee of final cost, and any change is priced in writing first.

Can you build in winter?

Some work can continue through winter with heat and enclosure, but foundations and concrete are best planned for the building season. We'll lay out a realistic schedule.

Do you offer design-build for an addition?

Yes. Edstrom offers design-build. We can work from your plans or help plan the addition, garage or shop, then build it.

Where do you build additions and garages?

North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright. For other Interior communities, ask about the location.
