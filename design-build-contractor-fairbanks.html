Local know-how

Plans, permits and engineering

Permit requirements depend on where your lot is. The City of Fairbanks and the City of North Pole have their own building requirements, and areas outside city limits differ. We confirm what applies before the design is final. Where a project needs stamped engineering, like foundations on difficult soils or larger commercial buildings, we bring in licensed engineers and coordinate their work into the plans.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is design-build?

One company handles both the design and the construction under one contract, instead of hiring a designer and then bidding the plans to builders.

Are you architects or engineers?

No. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor. When a project needs an architect or a stamped engineering design, we bring in licensed professionals and coordinate their work.

What kinds of projects do you design-build?

Custom homes, garages, shops, pole barns, additions, multifamily and commercial buildings in Fairbanks, North Pole and Interior Alaska.

Is the estimate a fixed price?

An estimate is not a guarantee of final cost. We price the design as it develops and put any changes in writing before the work is done.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster, and an estimate is not a guarantee of final cost.
