Where mold shows up in North Pole homes

Cold climate, tight houses and long winters create the same few problem spots.

Crawl spacesDamp soil, missing vapor barrier or an old leak under the floor.
AtticsFrost from warm, moist house air that melts and soaks insulation and sheathing.
Exterior wallsCondensation where warm indoor air meets cold sheathing.
After a water lossWet materials that weren't dried fast enough after a leak or backup.

Local know-how

Fix the cause, not just the stain

Wiping or painting over mold doesn't solve it. We locate the moisture source first, then contain the work area, remove mold-damaged materials, clean and dry the structure, and rebuild. Where the cause is ventilation, a vapor barrier or a roof detail, we explain the fix and price it in writing.

Questions

Straight answers.

Why does my North Pole attic have frost or mold?

Warm, moist air leaking from the house into a cold attic freezes on the roof sheathing in winter and melts in spring. Air sealing and ventilation usually fix the cause; damaged material is removed and replaced.

Are you IICRC certified?

Yes. Edstrom Construction is IICRC certified and holds Alaska contractor license CONG35843.

Does insurance pay for mold?

Some policies cover mold only when it results from a covered sudden loss, and many limit it. Your insurer decides. We document what we find.

Can I just bleach it?

Bleach doesn't fix the moisture and doesn't remove mold from porous materials like drywall and insulation. Those usually need to come out.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster, and an estimate is not a guarantee of final cost.
