The work

Kitchen remodeling

Edstrom Construction remodels homes in North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright. Every remodel gets a project manager, a written estimate and a crew that has built new homes from the slab up, so we know what's behind the wall before we open it. Bathroom remodeling

Insurance losses

Remodeling after water or fire damage

If a remodel starts with an insurance loss, the same crew can handle the water damage restoration or fire restoration and the upgrades. We bill insurance directly for covered work, and anything beyond the claim is priced separately in writing.

Coverage is decided by your policy and your insurer. A direct bill is not a promise that a claim will be paid.

Questions

Remodeling questions

Do you do small remodels?

Tell us what you have in mind. We'll be straight with you about whether it's a fit, and every project gets a written estimate.

Do I need a building permit for a remodel?

It depends on the address and the scope. The City of Fairbanks and the City of North Pole have their own building departments, and requirements differ outside city limits. We check what your property needs before work starts.

Where do you remodel?

North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright. For other Interior communities, ask about the location.

Do you offer design-build?

Yes. Edstrom offers design-build. You can bring plans, or we can help shape the plan and then build it. Either way you get a written estimate before we start.
