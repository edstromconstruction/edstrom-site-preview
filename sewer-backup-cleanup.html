What we do

Sewage cleanup is not a regular water job

A sewer backup is unsanitary water. We keep people out of the affected rooms, remove what the sewage touched, and do not close the walls until the structure is clean and dry.

Contain the lossWe separate the affected rooms so sewage is not tracked through the rest of the house.
Remove what sewage touchedCarpet, pad, wet drywall and other porous materials that contacted the backup come out. Hard surfaces that can be cleaned are cleaned.
Dry the structureAfter the contaminated material is gone, we dry the structure and check moisture before new finishes go in. If wet materials sat, see mold remediation.
Rebuild the roomsWe replace flooring, walls and fixtures the backup destroyed, then walk the finished rooms with you.

Basements and septic systems

Sewer or septic backup in the basement

Whether the house is on city sewer or a septic system, a backup into the basement, a floor drain or a bathroom is handled the same way inside the house: contain it, remove what it touched, clean, dry and rebuild. Edstrom cleans septic backups inside the home. A septic service clears the line or pumps the tank. We clean up and restore the house.

Insurance claims

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup?

Not every homeowners policy pays for a sewer backup. Some pay for it only when that coverage is on the policy. Your insurer decides. Edstrom still documents the loss, meets your adjuster on site when they inspect, and bills covered cleanup and repairs directly.

You choose the contractor. The insurer does not assign you to us, and we are not a public adjuster. We write a line-item estimate at current Alaska pricing and send a supplement if the first pass missed damaged material.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. A direct bill is not a promise that a claim will be paid, and an estimate is not a coverage decision. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster. If something is not covered, or you want an upgrade, we price that work in writing before we do it.

We bill insurance directly

We bill your insurance company directly for sewer backup cleanup and repairs that the claim covers. We do not promise that a policy includes sewer backup coverage.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is a sewer backup cleaned up differently than a clean-water leak?

Yes. Sewage is contaminated water. We keep the affected area separate, remove sewage and materials that cannot be saved, then clean and dry the structure before new finishes go in.

Do you offer 24/7 sewer backup cleanup?

Yes. Call (907) 488-0452. We respond 24/7 for sewer and septic backup cleanup in Fairbanks, North Pole and Fort Wainwright.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer backup?

Not every policy does. Some cover a sewer backup only when the policy includes that coverage. Your insurer decides. We document the loss and bill covered cleanup and repairs directly.

Do I have to use my insurance company's contractor?

No. You choose who cleans up and rebuilds. You can hire Edstrom Construction. We are a licensed Alaska contractor, CONG35843, and we are IICRC certified.

Can I clean up a sewer backup myself?

Keep people and pets out and don't run fans through the rest of the house. Sewage soaks into carpet, pad and drywall, which have to come out, not just be wiped. Call (907) 488-0452 and we'll tell you what to do until we arrive.
