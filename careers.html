Carpentry · Now hiring

Journeyman Carpenter

North Pole / Fairbanks, AK, plus remote Alaska job sitesCompensation based on experience

Lead and perform rough and finish carpentry on custom homes, multifamily, commercial, government and restoration projects in Fairbanks, North Pole and remote Alaska job sites.

What you'll do

  • Lay out and frame floors, walls and roofs; set trusses, sheathing, windows and doors
  • Build forms for footings, foundations and slabs
  • Install interior finish: trim, doors, cabinets, millwork and hardware
  • Read plans and specifications, take off materials and keep work on schedule
  • Frame metal studs, hang drywall and handle remodel and restoration rebuild work
  • Guide apprentices and laborers and keep the work area safe and clean
  • Take part in daily pre-task safety planning and follow the company safety program

Required

  • 4+ years of carpentry experience, or a completed carpentry apprenticeship
  • Skilled in both rough framing and finish carpentry
  • Able to read blueprints and lay out work with tape, square, level and laser
  • Own a full set of carpenter hand tools
  • Valid driver's license and reliable transportation to job sites
  • Able to lift 50 lb, climb ladders and scaffolding, and work at heights
  • Able to work outdoors in Interior Alaska conditions, including cold weather
  • Willing to travel to and stay at remote job sites when a project requires it
  • Pass pre-employment background and drug screening as allowed by law

Preferred

  • OSHA 10 or OSHA 30 card
  • First aid / CPR and fall protection training
  • Forklift, telehandler or aerial lift experience
  • Commercial, tenant improvement or government project experience
  • Fire and water restoration rebuild experience
  • Experience leading a small crew
  • Own power tools

Carpentry · Entry level · Now hiring

Carpenter Apprentice

North Pole / Fairbanks, AK, plus remote Alaska job sitesCompensation based on experience

An entry-level position for someone who wants to learn the carpentry trade. You will work beside our journeyman carpenters and learn framing, forming, finish work and job-site safety on real projects.

What you'll do

  • Help with framing, sheathing, concrete forms and finish work
  • Carry, stage and cut materials and keep tools organized
  • Do demolition and cleanup on remodel and restoration jobs
  • Learn to read plans, measure and lay out work
  • Wear required PPE and follow safety rules and directions on site

Required

  • 18 years of age or older
  • No experience needed; a willingness to learn and work hard
  • Shows up on time, ready to work, every day
  • Reliable transportation to job sites
  • Basic hand tools (tape measure, hammer, utility knife, tool belt), or willing to get them in your first 30 days
  • Able to lift 50 lb and work outdoors in all seasons
  • Pass pre-employment background and drug screening as allowed by law

Preferred

  • Valid driver's license
  • Shop class, vocational or trade school training
  • Any construction or labor experience
  • OSHA 10 card
  • Interest in growing into a journeyman carpenter
  • Willing to travel to remote job sites

Driving · CDL Class B · Now hiring

Class B CDL Driver

North Pole / Fairbanks, AK, plus remote Alaska job sitesCompensation based on experience

Drive straight trucks such as dump trucks, flatbeds and box trucks, hauling gravel, materials, equipment and debris between our North Pole yard, suppliers and job sites in the Fairbanks area.

What you'll do

  • Haul and deliver gravel, building materials, equipment and debris
  • Do pre-trip and post-trip inspections and complete vehicle inspection reports
  • Secure loads to FMCSA cargo securement rules
  • Keep accurate hours-of-service records
  • Report defects and help with basic truck upkeep
  • Help load and unload, and run a forklift or loader when qualified
  • Drive safely in winter, ice and low-light conditions

Required

  • Valid Class B CDL (or Class A) with no air brake restriction
  • Current DOT medical examiner's certificate
  • Acceptable driving record (MVR) under DOT and our insurance standards
  • 1+ year of verifiable commercial driving experience
  • Pass a DOT pre-employment drug test and take part in random DOT drug and alcohol testing
  • Consent to an FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse query
  • Able to do a full pre-trip inspection and secure loads
  • Able to lift 50 lb and work outdoors in Interior Alaska weather

Preferred

  • Dump truck experience
  • Tank vehicle (N) endorsement
  • Experience on gravel or rural Alaska roads
  • Forklift, loader or other equipment experience
  • Construction site hauling experience

Driving · CDL Class A · Now hiring

Class A CDL Driver

North Pole / Fairbanks, AK, plus remote Alaska job sitesCompensation based on experience

Drive tractor-trailer combinations, including lowboy, flatbed and dump trailers, hauling heavy equipment and materials across Interior Alaska and to remote and road-system projects.

What you'll do

  • Haul heavy equipment on a lowboy, including loading, unloading and chaining down
  • Haul materials on flatbed and dump trailers, with tarping and cargo securement
  • Do pre-trip and post-trip inspections and complete vehicle inspection reports
  • Keep hours-of-service records and plan trips around weather and road conditions
  • Coordinate oversize and overweight permits with the office when needed
  • Report defects and help with basic truck and trailer upkeep

Required

  • Valid Class A CDL with no air brake or manual transmission restriction
  • Current DOT medical examiner's certificate
  • 2+ years of verifiable Class A driving experience
  • Acceptable driving record (MVR) under DOT and our insurance standards
  • Pass a DOT pre-employment drug test and take part in random DOT drug and alcohol testing
  • Consent to an FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse query
  • Skilled with chains, binders and cargo securement
  • Safe winter driving experience, and able to take multi-day trips

Preferred

  • Lowboy or heavy-haul experience, including oversize loads
  • Flatbed, end dump or belly dump experience
  • Dalton, Parks, Richardson or other rural Alaska highway experience
  • Tanker (N) or doubles/triples (T) endorsement
  • HazMat (H) endorsement with TWIC card
  • Able to run a loader or excavator to load your own equipment

How hiring works

From application to your first day.

  1. 01

    Apply online

    Fill out the application for your position. A resume is welcome but not required.

  2. 02

    We review and call

    Our office reviews every application and calls qualified applicants.

  3. 03

    Interview

    Meet with a project manager to talk about your experience and the work.

  4. 04

    Offer & screening

    Offers are contingent on pre-employment background and drug screening as allowed by law. Drivers also complete a DOT driver application and qualification file.
