What we do

Emergency water extraction, drying and repair

The same company extracts the water, dries the building and does the repairs. Edstrom Construction is IICRC certified, and Alaska contractor license CONG35843.

Water extractionWe remove standing water from floors, basements and crawl spaces before it travels farther through the building.
Structural dryingWe dry the framing, subfloors and cavities the water reached, and we record moisture readings so we know when it is actually dry. Drying promptly keeps mold from starting.
Materials that can't be savedWet drywall, carpet, pad and insulation that will not dry cleanly come out. What can be saved stays.
The repairsWe replace the finishes we opened or removed: drywall, flooring, trim, cabinets and paint, built for an Interior Alaska house.

Basements and crawl spaces

Water in a basement or crawl space

Many Fairbanks and North Pole homes have crawl spaces or basements where water sits out of sight. We extract standing water there first, dry the framing, subfloor and insulation it reached, and record moisture readings so nothing gets closed up wet. Wet crawl-space insulation that can't dry cleanly comes out and is replaced.

Winter losses

Why water damage moves fast in an Interior Alaska winter

A burst supply line, a failed boiler or water heater, or an ice dam can put water into a house when it's 30 below and doors can't be left open to dry things out. That's why we set drying equipment instead of waiting for it to air out. If a frozen or burst pipe caused it, we coordinate the pipe repair through licensed plumbing partners and handle the water extraction, drying and rebuild.

Insurance claims

You choose who dries and rebuilds your home.

Your insurer does not pick the contractor. Edstrom works for you, the property owner. We photograph and measure the water damage, write a line-item estimate at current Alaska pricing, and bill your insurance company directly.

We will meet your adjuster on site and walk the damage. If the first estimate missed wet material or priced the work below local cost, we send a supplement with the photos and measurements behind it.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. A direct bill is not a promise that a claim will be paid, and an estimate is not a coverage decision. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster. If something is not covered, or you want an upgrade, we price that work in writing before we do it.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you respond to water damage 24/7 in Fairbanks and North Pole?

Yes. Call (907) 488-0452 any time. Edstrom Construction provides 24/7 emergency response for water damage in Fairbanks, North Pole and Fort Wainwright.

Do you bill insurance directly for water damage?

Yes. We bill your insurance company directly for the restoration work. Your policy and your insurer decide what is covered and what is paid. We do not guarantee coverage.

What does water damage restoration include?

We extract standing water, dry the structure, record moisture readings, remove materials that cannot be saved, and repair the building. Edstrom Construction is IICRC certified and holds Alaska contractor license CONG35843.

Can you rebuild after the dry-out?

Yes. The company that dries the home can repair drywall, flooring, cabinets and other finishes. If part of the work is not covered, we price that portion in writing before we do it.

How long does it take to dry out water damage?

It depends on how much water, how long it sat and what it soaked. We don't go by a set number of days. We take moisture readings and close walls only after the framing and subfloor read dry.
