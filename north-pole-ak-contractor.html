Local know-how

City water, private wells and septic

North Pole is two kinds of houses. Inside city limits, most homes are on the City of North Pole water and sewer utility. Outside city limits, along Badger Road, Hurst Road, Nordale Road and the side roads off them, most homes run on a private well and a septic system. That changes how a remodel is planned, how a backup is cleaned up and how a new garage or addition is laid out on the lot.

We plan the work around what is actually on your property, and we bring in licensed plumbing, electrical and septic companies for the parts of a job that call for them. Edstrom handles the construction and the coordination so you have one contact.

Questions

Straight answers.

Where is Edstrom Construction located?

Our shop is at 2135 Richardson Hwy, North Pole, AK 99705. We work throughout North Pole, Fairbanks and Fort Wainwright.

Do you work outside North Pole city limits?

Yes. A large share of our North Pole work is outside city limits, on homes with private wells and septic systems off Badger Road, Hurst Road, Plack Road and nearby roads.
