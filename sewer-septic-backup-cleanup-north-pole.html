Sewer and septic backup cleanup in North Pole, AlaskaCity sewer or septic tank.
We clean up the house.

North Pole homes back up two ways. Inside city limits, a blocked line between the house and the City of North Pole sewer main can push sewage up through a floor drain or toilet. Outside city limits, a full tank, a frozen line or a failed drain field can do the same thing. Either way, Edstrom cleans up inside the home, removes what the sewage touched, dries the structure and rebuilds the rooms.

Local know-how

Septic backups in winter

Outside city limits, many North Pole septic backups happen in deep cold, when a line or tank inlet freezes, or in spring, when a saturated drain field can't take water. Stop running water in the house until the tank or line is serviced, keep people and pets out of the affected area, and don't run fans that spread air from that room through the rest of the house.

Edstrom does not pump tanks or repair septic systems. We clean and restore the inside of the house after the septic company has done its part. Learn more on our main sewer and septic backup cleanup page.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you pump septic tanks?

No. A septic service pumps the tank and a qualified company repairs the system. Edstrom cleans up and restores the inside of the house.

Is a septic backup covered by homeowners insurance?

It depends on the policy and the cause. Some policies cover sewer or septic backup only when that coverage was added. Your insurer decides. We document the loss and bill covered work directly.

Can I stay in the house?

Often yes, if the backup is contained to one area and you have a working bathroom. Keep everyone out of the affected rooms. We'll tell you what we see when we arrive.

What gets thrown away?

Porous materials that sewage touched, like carpet, pad, drywall and insulation, usually have to come out. Hard surfaces can usually be cleaned.

Coverage and payment are decided by your policy and your insurance company. Edstrom Construction is a licensed general contractor, not a public adjuster, and an estimate is not a guarantee of final cost.
